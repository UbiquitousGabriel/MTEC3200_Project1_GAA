# Routine Audit (Part 1\) \- Gabriel Aguilar

**10:00 AM** — Wake up (work nights). *Mood: Groggy*

**11:00 AM** — Get ready for the day. *Mood: Groggy*

**12:00 PM** — Grab coffee or energy source. *Mood: Aware*

**1:00 PM** — Catch up on emails, texts, and social media. *Mood: Bored*

**2:00 PM – 3:00 PM** — Creative time: focus on personal projects. *Mood: Fulfilled*

**4:00 PM** — Commute to work. *Mood: Thoughtful*

**5:00 PM** — Start shift: set up stage, read, or work on creative projects.

**6:00 PM** — Client arrival: attend to needs and socialize lightly. *Mood: Socially Dissociating*

**7:00 PM** — Program show. *Mood: Smart*

**8:00 PM** — Show starts. *Mood: Stressed*

**11:00 PM** — Show ends: clean up. *Mood: Tired*

**12:00 AM** — Leave venue and head home. *Mood: Tired*

**1:00 AM** — Unwind and watch a show.
