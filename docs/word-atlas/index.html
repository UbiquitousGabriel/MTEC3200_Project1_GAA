Source: data/uua-readings-raw.json in the Chalice Reader repo, from UUA WorshipWeb and Braver/Wiser. pages had readable text. Built by npm run stats. Words are lowercased; common filler words and words under three letters are skipped. Phrases are counted by how many readings contain them; a few book titles and citations are left out (listed in scripts/word-stats.mjs). “Signature words” must show up 10+ times in 6+ readings of that type. Reading-aloud time assumes about 150 words a minute. The lookup covers every word used at least three times.
