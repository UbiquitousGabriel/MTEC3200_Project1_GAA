A metamorphosis may be  
majestic  
as when caterpillar becomes butterfly,  
brilliant  
as carbon to diamond.

But it is enough  
to release  
the piercing frigidity,  
the brittle rigidity  
of ice—

And embrace  
the versatile, vital,  
blessing, bathing,  
embodiment  
of water.
