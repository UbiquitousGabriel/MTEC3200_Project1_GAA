After each evocation, I invite you to respond with “we give thanks.”

For the books that stimulate our minds; open up our souls;  
make us laugh until we cry;  
and offer company when we are hurting, scared, or lonely—  
*We give thanks*.

For the books that remind us that someone else has faced this before us;  
books that connect us to others who have found comfort in their pages;  
and teach us empathy in a world of difference—  
*We give thanks*.

For the books that lead us to new ways of being and new faces for friendship;  
books that remind us of how the world used to be and how people throughout the ages have worked to make it better;  
and books that teach us how the world may be yet remade with greater justice, love, and compassion—  
*We give thanks*.

For the books that affirm the dignity and life of all,  
and scare those in power as they show us how to resist–  
*We give thanks*.

For all books, banned, welcomed, or never challenged,  
books that have offered wisdom and inspiration or fostered courage and transformation—  
*We give thanks*,

and may we be forever nourished by their words.

Amen and Blessed Be.
