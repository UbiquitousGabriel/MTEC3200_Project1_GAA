Is the fire going out?  
Not in your belly,  
for you are still alive,  
but in your soul,  
that place

where dreams  
fuel commitment

where longings  
shape action

where meaning  
flames purpose

where passion ignites  
and rekindles  
your life fire.

If your soul smolders  
dream on  
till you flame  
like a chalice of hope.
