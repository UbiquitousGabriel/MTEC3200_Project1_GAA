“When we welcome babies in our church, when we welcome new members into the community, when we celebrate the love of beaming couples, when we ordain new ministers, we speak not in the binding language of contract, but in the life-sustaining fluency of covenant, from *covenir*, to travel together. We will \[travel\] together with you, child; we will \[travel\] together with you, friend; we will \[travel\] together with each other toward the lives we mean to lead, toward the world we mean to have a hand in shaping, the world of compassion, equity, freedom, joy, and gratitude. Covenant is the work of intimate justice.”

—in “[Bound by Covenant](https://www.uuworld.org/articles/bound-covenant)”
