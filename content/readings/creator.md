We are all  
infinitesimal stitches  
knit into the shawl of God.

Her needles piece  
the mystery of the Universe,  
how Source arises from itself.  
Let your wonder take flight at the sharp  
miracle of breathing:  
Inhale—you are Creation.  
Exhale—you are Creator.
