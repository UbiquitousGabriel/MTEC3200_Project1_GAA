Fill me with anxiety, O Life!  
Electrify me, make me nervous  
Beyond any staid concern  
For those things which challenge  
Placid, flaccid ways, anachronisms of being.  
Keep me tense, a-tiptoe,  
Blinking at the novel,  
Reaching out for those things  
Just beyond my fingertips;  
So that I may make patterns,  
Dreams dreams, fashion worlds  
Which will beat with life.  
For I would be a man  
And on the move.
