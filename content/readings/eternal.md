It is not old,  
*Yet it comes through the wisdom of the ages.*  
It is not young,  
*Yet it comes through the passion of innocence.*  
It is not revolutionary,  
*Yet it comes proclaiming change.*  
It is not solitary,  
*Yet it travels alone, seeking the open heart.*  
It is not lonely,  
*Yet it seeks relationship.*  
It is not attached,  
*Yet it connects everything.*  
It is the fresh return of the eternal,  
*And it demands our response.*
