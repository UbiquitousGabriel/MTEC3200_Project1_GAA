These are the wonders of Earth-dwellers:  
How plants harvest sun and remake its heat into food,  
then is harnessed by the plant eaters and up: an infinitesimal wink  
of sun and Earth’s boiling core in each warm-blooded cell.  
  
Is it not a marvel?—How us Animal people  
marry oxygen to our red blood cells, how we release  
CO2 for the Plant people, and how we all  
must return our bodies back when shed of life.  
  
How can we not live in unity and awe within the  
symbiotic choreography of such miracles?
