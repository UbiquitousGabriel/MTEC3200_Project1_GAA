Each of us, imperfect and vulnerable bodyspirits that we are, is connected to a vast web of love which holds us and will not let us go. We are connected through our ancient common ancestor to creative lineages that shaped us. We are connected to the unborn, who still shine as stars in the sky. We spin this interdependent web from our very lives, as spiders spin fierce silk from their spinnerets. It will not let us go, no matter what we do or don’t do.

May you always know that you are held in this sacred web of love.
