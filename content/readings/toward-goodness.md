How can I hold fear,  
how can sadness dim the day  
when I step into the beauty of this  
gleaming new dawn?  
The fulcrum of the Earth tilts  
toward goodness and I will  
ride along, drinking deep the hours,  
worshiping as if this were my  
last chance.
