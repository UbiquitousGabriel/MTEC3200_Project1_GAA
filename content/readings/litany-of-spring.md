As we gather to celebrate  
In this time of beginnings  
In the season of birth and rebirth,  
We hear the heart beat of Mother Earth

*With the spring time comes the blossoming of the plants  
A time of exploration and creation  
And the emerging patterns of life  
We hear the heart beat of Mother Earth*

Let us draw closer to each other and our creator  
In this the time of reflection and growth  
In the continuing circle of life  
We hear the heart beat of Mother Earth

*We recognize strengths and struggles in community  
We give thanks for gifts of kindness and understanding  
And our emotions with the spirit  
We hear the heart beat of Mother Earth*

**Unison** Let us remember past and present  
And the start of the week we begin anew  
Let us share laughter, love and joy  
We hear the heart beat of Mother Earth
