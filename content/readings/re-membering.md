We make ourselves one body  
Something larger than we were before  
With all that feels alive in me  
And all that feels alive in you  
We are interconnected  
We are one entity  
Having all these divergent, disparate,  
seemingly contentious experiences,  
but still part of this one body.  
Here we are conspiring together.  
Here we are breathing together.  
Here we are moving together,  
moving for the sake of our whole body.

Because that’s who we are.

This is a reunion.  
We’re re-membering.  
Re-Membering.
