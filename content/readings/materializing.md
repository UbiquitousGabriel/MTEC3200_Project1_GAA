In what form did the spirit  
appear to you today,  
the blossom of a flower,  
the tug of a child’s hand,  
the silent twinkling stars,  
an old woman smiling at the bus stop,  
a lover’s ­gentle hug,  
a presence so close to your soul  
you ­could almost touch it,  
words of truth formed  
unbidden in your mind?

The holy disguised in so many ways,  
may your senses open wide in recognition.
