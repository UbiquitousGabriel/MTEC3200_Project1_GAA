Out of the depths I cry: Who will hear my voice? Out of the pain I cry: Who will make space for my living and my dying? Out of the years I cry: Who will remember? Who will remember? My soul awaits: *More than those who watch for the morning. More than those who watch for the morning.*

—inspired by Psalm 130 and Maya Angelou
