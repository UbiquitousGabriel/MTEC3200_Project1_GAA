We light this chalice to honor the spark of life  
that dwells within each body present;  
recognizing the fullness of our shapes and sizes  
are countless as stars in an infinite universe.

Like this chalice, may each spark burn:  
fierce as our resistance to oppression  
bright as our hopes for a better tomorrow  
strong as the love that holds all our bodies  
within the care of this beloved community.
