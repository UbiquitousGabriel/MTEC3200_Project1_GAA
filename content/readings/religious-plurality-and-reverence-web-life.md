*from Rev. Dr. Sheri Prud’homme’s* [*“Theological Reflection on the Proposed Revision to Article II” (PDF, 9 pages)*](https://www.uua.org/files/2024-01/Article%20II%20Theological%20Reflection%201-22-24.pdf)
