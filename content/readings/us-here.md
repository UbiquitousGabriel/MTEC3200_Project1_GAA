This litany is intended to be read antiphonally (the congregation, split left and right, reading it to one another).

Here I am,  
my sibling.  
Here I am.

Can you  
hear me?  
Here I am.

Saying you …  
You. Here.  
Now. We.

We are.  
Here. Us.  
Together.

Can you.  
Hear me  
here? I am

calling to you.  
Calling to  
us. You. Me.

Us together.  
Here I am.  
Calling. Let’s

all say “yes.”  
Let’s. Here.  
Now. Together.

All say …  
Yes!
