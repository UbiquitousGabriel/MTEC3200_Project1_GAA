I trust that my heart knows the way,  
I trust my will to hold,  
I trust that I am to go,  
I trust that this is the way,  
I trust that the obstacles will move, or be scalable.  
I trust myself,  
I trust Spirit, the trees, the oceans, the sky and the stars.  
I trust the way.
