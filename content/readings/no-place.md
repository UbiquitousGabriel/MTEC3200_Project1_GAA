There is not a place  
for everything.  
*Hate, racism, corporate greed,*  
*the spoiling of Mother Earth.*

Everything is not  
in its place.  
*Justice, equality,*  
*freedom, compassion.*  
*Love.*

The place is within our  
collective hearts and minds.  
*Where do you keep your love?*  
*Invite love in.*  
*Make a place for love.*  
*The others will follow.*
