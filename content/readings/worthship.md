Words of worth  
rebound, resound,  
walls cannot hold  
the melody, the song  
igniting fires, hearts  
in motion, emotion  
not alone, evolving  
into revolution,  
into action  
for each one in the world  
feeling alone,  
unknown, reaching  
to the root,  
the radical source  
Love.
