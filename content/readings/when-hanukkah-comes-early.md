Stories of guerrilla warfare and forbidden learning  
need witness  
by small flames that grow in number  
We marvel at the miracles of oil and bodies  
and fry potatoes until it smells like the holy day it is  
It’s the First Night of the rest of our lives!  
We could have assimilated but instead  
we rededicate
