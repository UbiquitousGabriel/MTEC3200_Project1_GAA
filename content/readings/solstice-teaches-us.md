The Solstices teach us  
that darkness comes,  
that darkness goes.

The Solstices teach us  
that light comes,  
that light goes.

The Solstices teach us,  
calmly, silently, to be  
calm, silent. Learning.

The Solstices teach us  
as we circle the sun  
that everything flies.

The Solstices teach us  
to remember the dark,  
to remember the light,

to remember time.  
The seasons. And love  
as we circle the sun.
