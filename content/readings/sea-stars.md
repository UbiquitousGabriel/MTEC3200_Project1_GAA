Our ancestry arose in the ancient seas, first lifeforms drinking light.  
Our family evolved: senses, roots, limbs, scales, minds.  
We raced across the world in glorious green,  
stretched aloft the canopy, spread wings to take the skies.  
We made simple tools to shape the world,  
befriended our enemy, fire,  
and painted horses on the cave wall.  
We sang, we loved, we gazed up at the mysterious stars.  
We tried to understand what had created us, why we were here,  
why we suffered and how to keep what was precious.  
Once again we raced across the world,  
changed colors, befriended the field and the wolf.  
We built shelters which grew to palaces.  
We fought and died  
yet still we sang. Still we mapped the constellations.  
Our ancestry arose in the ancient sea  
of stars.
