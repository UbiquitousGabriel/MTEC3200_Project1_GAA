The road not taken  
is not a fork. That’s  
too simple a thought,  
flipping some coin.

Either/or. Nope. It’s  
not this or that.  
No or yes. It’s nor/

neither and all  
of the above and  
more. The road  
taken goes every

way at once. There.  
There. They. Them.  
Go. Now. Live.
